## Business contacts we write to

We sometimes email businesses that we believe could use our products, such as freight forwarders who may benefit from Kazinga. To do this we collect a company's name, website and published business email address, and where the company publishes them, a contact's name and job title. We take these only from sources the company makes public, such as its own website or an official trade directory, and each email tells you where we found the address.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in telling businesses about relevant products (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR and EU GDPR). We send one email and at most one follow-up. If you reply asking us to stop, we will not contact you again and we keep only your address on a do-not-contact list so that we can respect that. If you do not reply, we delete your details 12 months after our last email.

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to this use, by writing to hello@tiph.agency. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.
