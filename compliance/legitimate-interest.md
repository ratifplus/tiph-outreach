# Legitimate interest assessment: B2B prospecting

Controller: Tiph Ltd, 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX (Company No. 17199579). Contact: hello@tiph.agency. Prepared 18 October 2026.

## 1. Purpose
Contact freight forwarders about Kazinga, software that turns incoming rate requests into quotes for a person to check and send. Tiph has a commercial interest in finding customers; recipients may benefit from faster quoting. Direct marketing is recognised as a possible legitimate interest (UK/EU GDPR Recital 47).

## 2. Necessity
We need a business address to make contact. We collect the minimum: company name, website, the published business email, and where published a contact name and role. No personal social media, no private addresses, no sensitive data, no tracking pixels.

## 3. Balancing
- Recipients are businesses whose published addresses exist so the public can contact them about their services.
- Messages are relevant to their job, individually written, low frequency (one email and at most one follow-up).
- Each email says who we are, where we found the address, and how to opt out; the privacy notice explains the rest.
- Opt-outs are honoured immediately and kept on a suppression list.
- Data is held in a private repository and in the Zoho mailbox (EU data centre); non-responders are deleted after 12 months.

Conclusion: the interest is legitimate, the processing is necessary and the impact on individuals is low. Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) applies, subject to the ePrivacy rules per country in `jurisdictions.md`.

## 4. Review
Review if volume rises above 1,000 emails per month, if complaints exceed 1 in 500 emails, or if a new market is added.
